# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars
# AIM
To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.
# THEORY
Self-driving cars, also known as autonomous vehicles, are intelligent vehicles that use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to operate with little or no human intervention. These vehicles use advanced technologies such as Machine Learning (ML), Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Sensor Fusion, and Path Planning to understand their surroundings and make driving decisions.

Self-driving cars are equipped with various sensors, including cameras, LiDAR, radar, ultrasonic sensors, and GPS. These sensors collect information about the vehicle's surroundings, such as roads, pedestrians, other vehicles, traffic signals, and obstacles.

Computer Vision is an important AI technology used in self-driving cars. It helps the vehicle analyze images captured by cameras and identify objects such as road signs, lane markings, pedestrians, and other vehicles.

Machine Learning and Deep Learning enable autonomous vehicles to learn from large amounts of driving data. These technologies help the vehicle recognize different road conditions, predict the movement of other vehicles, and improve its driving performance.

Sensor Fusion combines information collected from different sensors to create a more accurate understanding of the surrounding environment. For example, cameras identify objects, while radar measures their distance and relative speed. LiDAR helps create a three-dimensional representation of the surroundings.

Path Planning and Decision-Making help the vehicle determine a safe route and select suitable driving actions. The AI system decides when to accelerate, brake, turn, change lanes, or stop based on road conditions and traffic situations.

Navigation Systems use GPS and digital maps to determine the vehicle's location and guide it toward the destination. AI algorithms help select efficient routes and respond to changing traffic conditions.

The working of a self-driving car involves three major stages: perception, decision-making, and control. In the perception stage, sensors collect environmental data. In the decision-making stage, AI analyzes the data and selects an appropriate action. In the control stage, the vehicle's steering, braking, and acceleration systems execute the selected action.

Self-driving cars are used in autonomous transportation, intelligent traffic management, delivery services, and passenger mobility. They have the potential to improve road safety, reduce human errors, and provide efficient transportation.

However, self-driving cars face challenges such as unpredictable road conditions, bad weather, sensor errors, complex traffic situations, and cybersecurity risks. Therefore, extensive testing and safety systems are necessary before autonomous vehicles can operate reliably.

Thus, Artificial Intelligence plays an important role in self-driving cars by enabling vehicles to perceive their surroundings, make intelligent decisions, and navigate roads with reduced human intervention.

# RESULT
Thus, the application of Artificial Intelligence in self-driving cars was studied successfully. The role of Computer Vision, Machine Learning, Sensor Fusion, Path Planning, and Navigation Systems in autonomous driving was understood. The study demonstrated how AI technologies help vehicles identify obstacles, make driving decisions, and navigate safely with reduced human intervention.
